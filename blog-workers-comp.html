Workers Compensation Physiotherapy in NSW: What You Need to Know

If you've been injured at work in NSW, physiotherapy is often a key part of getting you back on your feet, and in most cases it won't cost you anything out of pocket. Here's what you need to know.

You can choose your own physiotherapist

Under the NSW workers compensation scheme, you have the right to choose your own treating physiotherapist. You are not limited to a provider chosen by your employer or their insurer. If you'd prefer to be treated locally in Dapto, you can ask for that.

How the process works

  1. Report your injury to your employer and see your GP, who documents it and issues a Certificate of Capacity.
  2. A claim is lodged with the insurer and you're given a claim number.
  3. You start physiotherapy, bringing your claim number and insurer details to your first visit.
  4. We prepare a treatment plan and seek insurer approval for ongoing sessions where needed.
  5. Approved treatment is billed directly to the insurer.

Common workplace injuries we treat

Back and neck pain from lifting or repetitive strain, shoulder and wrist injuries, knee and ankle sprains, and muscle strains are among the most common. Early treatment generally leads to better outcomes.
