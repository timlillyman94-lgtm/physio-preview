Your Physiotherapy Questions, Answered

We get asked the same handful of questions all the time. Here are honest answers to the most common ones.

Do I need a referral to see a physio?

No. You can book directly with us as a private patient. Referrals are only needed for certain funding pathways, such as a GP care plan, WorkCover or some insurance claims.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition and how long you've had it. After your first assessment we'll give you an honest estimate and track your progress rather than keep you coming back indefinitely.

Does treatment hurt?

Most treatment is comfortable. Some hands-on techniques or dry needling can feel a little tender, but we always work within your comfort and explain what we're doing.

Will I get exercises to do at home?

Usually, yes. The right exercises are often what makes your results last, and we'll make sure they're clear and manageable.

Do you treat WorkCover and NDIS patients?

Yes, along with private patients. Just bring your claim number or plan details and we'll handle the rest.
