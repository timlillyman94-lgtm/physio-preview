The clinic

Working at Functional Physiotherapy

Functional Physiotherapy was founded in 2022. Our Dapto clinic is inside Dapto Medical Professionals, on the Princes Highway.

We provide one-on-one physiotherapy for sports and work injuries, joint and muscle conditions, vestibular presentations and disability-related support. You can read about our work on the services page and find out more about Chris on the team page.
