Careers

Physiotherapy jobs in Dapto and the Illawarra

We are growing, and we would like to hear from physiotherapists who want to treat properly — one-on-one appointments, time to assess, and support to develop rather than a conveyor belt.

Home/Careers
The pitch

What working here is like

A small, established clinic in a part of the Illawarra that is growing quickly.

Functional Physiotherapy has been treating the Dapto community for more than eight years from a clinic inside Dapto Medical Professionals, on the Princes Highway. The caseload is genuinely varied: sporting injuries from local clubs, work injuries from the region’s industrial and care sectors, spinal pain, vestibular presentations, NDIS participants and post-surgical rehabilitation.

Appointments are one-on-one and long enough to do the job. We do not double-book, we do not hand patients to an assistant halfway through, and we do not measure a good day by how many bodies passed through the room. If you have worked somewhere that did, you will know why that is worth saying out loud.

West Dapto is one of the largest residential growth areas in the state, which means the local population and the demand for good physiotherapy are both increasing. There is room here to build a caseload around what you are actually interested in.

What we offer

What you get

  • A one-on-one caseload with realistic appointment lengths
  • Mentoring and structured professional development support
  • Scope to build a special interest — sports, vestibular, workers comp, NDIS
  • A mixed caseload rather than one funding stream
  • Monday to Friday hours — no weekend roster
  • An established local referral base and a strong community reputation
What we look for

What we are after

  • AHPRA registration as a physiotherapist (or eligibility for it)
  • New graduates and experienced clinicians both welcome
  • Genuine interest in assessment and reasoning, not just technique
  • Willingness to explain things properly to patients
  • Interest in local sport, community work or a clinical special interest
  • Someone who wants to stay — we are building a team, not filling a gap
Open to

Roles we are interested in talking about

No formal vacancy list — we would rather hear from the right person and build a role around them.

Physiotherapist — full time

A full caseload across the clinic’s mix of private, sporting, WorkCover and NDIS work.

Physiotherapist — part time

Set days or school hours. We would rather have the right clinician part time than the wrong one full time.

New graduate

Structured mentoring, longer appointment times while you find your feet, and a supervisor who is actually in the building.

How to apply

Get in touch

There is no formal application process and no portal to fight with. Email a CV and a few lines about what you are looking for to info@functionalphysio.com.au, or call the clinic on (02) 4221 9637 and ask to speak with Chris.

If you are a student or new graduate wanting to look around first, that is fine too — say so and we will organise a time.
