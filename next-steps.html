About this preview & what happens next

This is a concept redesign of the Functional Physiotherapy website. It is a working preview to look at and click through, not the live site. Nothing here is public.

How to look through it

Start at the home page and click around using the menu. Every page is real and links to the others. The "Book now" buttons open the actual Cliniko booking portal. Try it on your phone as well as a computer, the whole thing is built to work properly on mobile.

What's included

  • Home, Services overview, and a page for each priority service: WorkCover, Sports Injury, Neck & Back Pain, Dry Needling, NDIS, and Headaches & Vertigo
  • About / team, Contact, and a Blog with three articles
  • Consistent branding, mobile layout, and the same booking link as the current site

What's real vs. placeholder

  • Photos are the clinic's own, pulled from the current website (treatment, clinic and team shots). Fresh professional photography of the Dapto clinic and team would lift it further, but these are all real.
  • Service page wording is a first draft written to be accurate and helpful. Chris should review and adjust it to match how the clinic talks about each service.
  • Team page lists Chris only, as a placeholder. Add each physiotherapist with their correct photo and a short bio, and confirm the headshot shown matches the right person.
  • Blog posts are representative versions; the final site would carry over the real posts.

Open questions for Chris

  • Can we arrange fresh professional photography of the Dapto clinic and team?
  • Any changes to the service wording or the services offered?
  • Confirm the full team list and match each name to the right headshot.

What it would take to make this the real, live site

This preview is a set of local files. Turning it into the live website is a proper (but small) project. The main steps and dependencies:

  1. Decide the platform. Rebuild on a modern hosted stack (recommended: a fast static site plus a simple content system so Chris can still edit words and add posts), or WordPress. This preview is the design either way.
  2. Finalise content and photos. Confirm the service wording and, ideally, add fresh professional photography.
  3. Set up the content system so Chris can edit text, swap photos and publish blog posts himself.
  4. Rebuild the forms. The contact form is simple; the health-declaration form collects health information and needs a proper, private setup.
  5. Protect the search rankings. Keep every page address the same where possible, redirect anything that changes, and carry over all page titles and descriptions. This is the most important technical step, the site currently ranks well and we must not lose that.
  6. Test on a staging link, then go live by pointing the domain to the new host.
  7. After launch: update the website link on the Google Business Profile, re-confirm Google Search Console, and monitor rankings closely for about a month.

Rough size

  • Effort: around 1–2 weeks of build work, most of which is already designed here.
  • Ongoing cost: similar to, or cheaper than, the current Wix plan.
  • Main risk: a temporary dip in Google rankings during the move, managed with careful redirects.
