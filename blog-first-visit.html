What to Expect at Your First Physiotherapy Appointment

If you've never seen a physiotherapist, the first visit can feel like a bit of an unknown. Here's what actually happens, so you can walk in knowing what to expect.

A thorough assessment

We start by talking through your problem, your history and your goals. Then we assess how you move, testing the areas involved, so we can understand what's really driving your pain or restriction.

An explanation you can understand

We'll explain what we've found in plain language, what's likely going on, and what the plan looks like. You should leave your first appointment understanding your condition and the path forward.

Treatment starts on day one

In most cases we begin treatment straight away, which may include hands-on therapy, dry needling and some initial exercises to get you started.

What to bring

  • Any relevant scans, referrals or reports
  • Comfortable clothing you can move in
  • For WorkCover or NDIS, your claim number or plan details

That's it. No mystery, just a clear assessment and a plan built around you.
