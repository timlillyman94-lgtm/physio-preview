Dry Needling (GEMT)

Dry needling in Dapto

Dry needling targets tight, painful muscles to release tension and restore movement. We use it alongside hands-on therapy and exercise as part of a complete treatment plan.

Dry needling uses fine, sterile needles to release trigger points, tight bands of muscle that can cause pain and restrict how you move. It's performed by our physiotherapists as part of a broader plan, not on its own, so you get lasting relief rather than a temporary fix.

What we treat

What dry needling can help

  • Muscle tightness and trigger points
  • Neck and back pain
  • Sports and overuse injuries
  • Tension-type and neck-related headaches
  • Restricted or painful movement
Our approach

How we use it

  • Assessment first to find the source of your pain
  • Dry needling combined with hands-on treatment
  • GEMT technique performed by qualified physios
  • Paired with exercise so results last
  • Clear explanation of what to expect at every step
Good to know

Dry needling FAQs

Does dry needling hurt?
Most people feel only a small prick, sometimes a brief muscle twitch or ache. It's generally well tolerated, and we adjust to your comfort.
Is dry needling the same as acupuncture?
No. Dry needling is based on modern anatomy and targets muscular trigger points, whereas acupuncture is based on traditional Chinese medicine principles.
Is it safe?
Yes, when performed by a trained physiotherapist using sterile, single-use needles. We'll talk through any questions before we start.
How many sessions will I need?
Dry needling is usually part of a broader plan. Many people notice a difference within a few sessions; we'll track your response and adjust.
