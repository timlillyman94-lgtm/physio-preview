Good to know

NDIS physio FAQs

How is NDIS physiotherapy funded?
Physiotherapy is generally funded under Improved Daily Living (Capacity Building) in your plan. We'll help you understand how it applies to you.
Which plan management types do you accept?
We work with plan-managed, self-managed and agency-managed participants. Let us know which applies and we'll sort out the paperwork.
Do you offer home visits?
Yes. We can see participants at home across Dapto and the wider Illawarra where that's the best setting for your goals.
Do I need a referral?
You don't need a GP referral, but it helps to have your NDIS plan and goals handy so we can align treatment from the first visit.
