How it works

Workers compensation physiotherapy in NSW

The NSW scheme, regulated by SIRA and administered through icare and scheme agents, covers physiotherapy for work-related injuries. Here's the path from injury to recovery.

01

Report your injury

Tell your employer and see your GP, who documents the injury and issues a Certificate of Capacity.

02

A claim is lodged

Your employer's insurer opens a claim and issues you a claim number.

03

Start physiotherapy

Bring your claim number and insurer details to your first visit. Early treatment leads to better outcomes.

04

Your treatment plan

We assess your injury, set clear goals and, where required, seek insurer approval for ongoing sessions.

05

We bill the insurer

Approved treatment is invoiced directly to your insurer, with no upfront payment from you.

06

Return to work

We work with your GP, employer and case manager toward safe, sustainable duties.

What we treat

Work injuries we help with

  • Lower back and neck pain from lifting, manual handling or repetitive strain
  • Shoulder, elbow, wrist and hand injuries, including RSI and overuse
  • Knee, ankle and foot injuries, sprains and strains
  • Post-surgical rehabilitation after a workplace injury
  • Muscle strains, soft-tissue and crush or impact injuries
Our approach

Focused on getting you back to work

  • Thorough initial assessment with clear, measurable recovery goals
  • Graded exercise and strengthening built around your job demands
  • Hands-on manual therapy and dry needling for pain and mobility
  • Education to manage your injury and prevent re-injury
  • Ongoing communication with your GP, employer and case manager
Good to know

Workers compensation physio FAQs

Do I have to pay for workers compensation physiotherapy?
For an approved WorkCover claim, we bill your insurer directly for treatment, so there's no upfront cost to you.
Can I choose my own physiotherapist for a work injury in NSW?
Yes. Under NSW workers compensation you have the right to choose your treating physiotherapist. You can request Functional Physio in Dapto.
What if my claim hasn't been approved yet?
Get in touch and we'll explain what's needed and how we can help you get started while the paperwork is finalised.
How many physiotherapy sessions does WorkCover cover?
It depends on your injury and is guided by your treatment plan and insurer approval. We'll keep you informed at every stage.
Which areas do you service for WorkCover physio?
We're based in Dapto and see injured workers from across the Illawarra including Wollongong, Shellharbour, Unanderra, Horsley, Kanahooka, Berkeley and surrounds.
