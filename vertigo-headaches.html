Good to know

Headache & vertigo FAQs

Can physiotherapy really help vertigo?
Yes. For common causes like BPPV, specific repositioning techniques are highly effective, often within one or two sessions. Other causes respond well to vestibular rehabilitation.
What is BPPV?
BPPV is a very common cause of positional vertigo, where brief spinning is triggered by head movements. It's caused by tiny crystals in the inner ear and is very treatable.
Do you treat migraines?
We treat the neck-related pain and stiffness that can accompany migraine. Migraine itself is best co-managed with your GP; we focus on the physical contributors we can help.
Is the treatment safe?
Yes. Assessment and treatment are gentle and evidence-based. We'll explain each step and check in with how you're feeling throughout.
