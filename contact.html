Before your first appointment

What to bring

Comfortable clothing you can move in, plus anything relevant to how you are paying — private health card, Medicare referral, WorkCover claim number or NDIS plan details.

How long it takes

Allow around 45 minutes for an initial consultation. Follow-up appointments are usually around 30 minutes.
