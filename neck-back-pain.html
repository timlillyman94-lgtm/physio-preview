Good to know

Neck and back pain FAQs

Do I need a scan before I see a physio?
Usually not. Most neck and back pain is diagnosed through a physical assessment. We'll advise you if imaging is genuinely needed.
Can physiotherapy help sciatica?
Yes. Physiotherapy is a first-line treatment for most sciatica, using a combination of hands-on treatment, nerve mobility work and progressive exercise.
How many sessions will I need?
It varies with how long you've had the pain and its cause. We'll give you an honest estimate after your first assessment and track your progress.
Is it safe to exercise with back pain?
In most cases, yes, and the right movement actually speeds recovery. We'll give you exercises matched to your stage and comfort.
