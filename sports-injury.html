Whether it's an acute injury or a niggle that won't settle, sports physiotherapy works best when treatment and rehabilitation go hand in hand. We find the cause, settle your symptoms with hands-on treatment, and rebuild your strength and load tolerance so the injury doesn't come back. Every plan is built around your sport and your goals.

What we treat

Sporting injuries we treat

  • Hamstring, calf and quad strains
  • Ankle sprains and instability
  • Knee injuries, including ligament, meniscus and kneecap pain
  • Shoulder injuries and rotator cuff problems
  • Tendon injuries (Achilles, patellar, rotator cuff)
  • Overuse and load-related injuries
  • Post-surgical sports rehabilitation
Our approach

How we get you back to sport

  • Accurate diagnosis and load assessment
  • Hands-on therapy and dry needling to settle symptoms
  • Progressive strength and conditioning
  • Sport-specific return-to-play testing
  • Injury prevention and movement screening
Good to know

Sports physio FAQs

How soon after an injury should I see a physio?
The sooner the better. Early assessment helps us protect the injury, settle pain and start the right rehabilitation, which usually means a faster, safer return to sport.
Do I need a referral to see a sports physio?
No. You can book directly as a private patient. If you're claiming through a GP care plan, WorkCover or insurance, bring the relevant paperwork.
Do you do return-to-sport testing?
Yes. We use sport-specific tests to make sure you're genuinely ready to return, rather than guessing, which lowers your risk of re-injury.
Can you help me avoid injuries in the first place?
Yes. Movement screening and a targeted strength and conditioning program can reduce your risk, especially for recurring or load-related injuries.
